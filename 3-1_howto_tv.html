How to use Sunlight TV

Sunlight TV is connected to my Are.na channel, where I archive photos taken using a solar panel as the power source. You can browse these photos on the TV or watch the music video made from them.
Ready to watch? Click the ☀︎ Sunlight TV button in the middle of the website!

**

These are the main buttons.
Hit ☀︎ button to turn on the TV. The music video will play simultaneously.
You can pause the video by hitting the ☀︎ button again.


This field allows you to watch your Are.na channel on Sunlight TV.
Copy and paste the URL of your Are.na channel into this field and hit Enter.
Your Are.na channel must be public, not private.


**

If you're interested in synchronizing the photos with the music more precisely, you can add a <div> with a specific class to an Are.na block's description.

For example, you can add a <div> like the ones below to an Are.na block's description. You can also combine multiple classes, such as,

<div class="middle mute"></div>

<div class="short"></div>
Displays the block for 1.5 seconds

<div class="middle"></div>
Displays the block for 6 seconds

<div class="long"></div>
Displays the block for 9 seconds

<div class="mute"></div>
Mutes the video's audio
